## Factors Affecting SSD Access Time

1. **NAND Type**: SLC, MLC, TLC, and QLC flash memory have different speeds. SLC is fastest, QLC is slower.
2. **Controller Quality**: Advanced controllers optimize data retrieval and wear leveling.
3. **Caching**: DRAM or SLC caches can reduce effective access time.
4. **Interface**: NVMe over PCIe is faster than SATA due to higher bandwidth and lower latency
